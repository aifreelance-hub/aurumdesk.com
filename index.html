Terms & Service Policy

01. Pricing & Logistics

1. Regional Surcharges: Listed prices do not apply to islands or remote areas. If the delivery address is in an island or remote region, the customer must pay the actual freight price difference.

2. Shipping Scope: We only support delivery to countries listed. Orders outside our shipping scope cannot be fulfilled. USA: Delivery is limited to the Continental US only. We CANNOT ship to: AK (Alaska), HI (Hawaii), PR (Puerto Rico), GU (Guam), and AP (Armed Forces Pacific).

3. European Warehouse Policy: Our European facility does not accept return services. Please confirm all details before shipping. Failed deliveries will be returned to the origin at the customer's expense.

4. Address Modifications: Changing an address in transit incurs an operational fee. Redirects are not guaranteed to succeed; fees remain non-refundable regardless of the outcome.

02. Delivery Issues

• In-Transit Interception: Requesting an interception requires the customer to bear interception fees plus two-way shipping costs (return freight equals outbound freight).

• Refusal of Delivery: For groundless refusal, the customer must bear all shipping costs. Refunds are only processed after warehouse inspection confirms no impact on secondary sales. COD returns are not accepted.

• Lost Packages: Defined as tracking being inactive for an extended period. To claim, you MUST provide a full-page English screenshot of the order (partial screenshots are invalid) showing the address, tracking number, and value. Full refund or reshipment is available only after a successful claim.

• Delivered but Not Received: Without "Signature Service," we cannot provide claims. We only provide POD (Proof of Delivery). Investigations require customer evidence (e.g., surveillance footage) and are not guaranteed to succeed.

03. Returns & Quality

• Damage Upon Arrival: Please refuse delivery if the package is destroyed. If signed for, you must provide photos of the outer box, shipping label, and product damage within 7 days. Claims made after unauthorized returns will not be refunded if the item is damaged.

• TPE Characteristics: Dolls are medical-grade TPE. Facial makeup is hand-painted and can wear off during use; this is not a quality defect.

• Skeleton Limits: Standard skeletons are used. Do not force joints beyond natural human limits. Refer to the manual for posing guidance.

• Feedback Deadline: Quality issues must be reported within 7 days of receipt. Claims filed after this period will not be handled.

04. Hygiene & Special Sale

PRIVATE HEALTH PRODUCTS: Due to hygiene regulations, returns or exchanges for non-quality reasons are strictly NOT accepted. Quality issues are resolved via reshipment of parts or compensation only.

• Unauthorized Returns: If a package is returned without authorization, no compensation or refund will be provided if the warehouse cannot locate the item or it impacts secondary sales.

FULL SILICONE PRODUCTS: All silicone products are strictly non-returnable and non-exchangeable. Quality issues are eligible for parts reshipment or compensation only.
