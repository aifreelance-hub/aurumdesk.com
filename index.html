Shipping Policy

Price & Delivery Notice

  • • Additional shipping fees may apply for remote areas, islands, or special delivery regions.

  • • European warehouse addresses do not accept return packages under any circumstances.
  • • US shipping is limited to the mainland United States only. We do NOT ship to Alaska (AK), Hawaii (HI), Puerto Rico (PR), Guam (GU), or Armed Forces Pacific (AP) military addresses.

Undelivered Packages

  • • Customers requesting shipment interception are responsible for interception fees and round-trip shipping costs.
  • • Refused deliveries without valid reason may require customers to pay both outbound and return shipping fees.
  • • Lost or abnormal shipments require full English screenshots including delivery address, tracking number, and order value for claim processing.
  • • Address change requests during transit may incur additional carrier processing fees and cannot be guaranteed successful.
  • • If tracking shows delivered but customer claims non-receipt, investigations require supporting evidence such as security footage or delivery proof requests.
Return Conditions

Refund & Return Policy

Product Damage

  • • Customers must refuse packages with visible severe external damage before signing.
  • • After delivery, customers must provide: outer box photos, shipping label photos, and detailed product damage photos.
  • • Depending on damage severity, compensation, replacement, or full refund may be offered.
  • • Unauthorized returns may not qualify for refunds if products are damaged or unsuitable for resale.

Important Material Notice

  • • TPE facial makeup is hand-applied and natural wear over time is not considered a product defect.
  • • Standard skeleton structures should not be forced beyond normal movement limitations.
  • • Product quality issues must be reported within 7 days after delivery.
  • • Due to hygiene regulations, private health products are NOT eligible for unconditional returns or exchanges.
  • • Silicone products are strictly non-returnable and non-exchangeable.
