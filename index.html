Shipping & Return Policy

Price & Delivery Notice

  • • Additional shipping charges may apply for islands, remote regions, or special delivery zones.
  • • Orders can only be shipped to supported countries and regions. Addresses outside our supported delivery network cannot be fulfilled.
  • • European warehouse returns are NOT accepted. Please confirm your shipping information carefully before dispatch.
  • • US warehouse shipping is limited to the mainland United States only. We do NOT ship to Alaska (AK), Hawaii (HI), Puerto Rico (PR), Guam (GU), or Armed Forces Pacific (AP) addresses.

After-Sales Policy

  • • If a package is intercepted during transit, customers are responsible for interception fees and round-trip shipping costs.
  • • Refused deliveries without valid reason require customers to cover return shipping costs. Refunds are only issued after warehouse inspection.
  • • Lost packages or abnormal tracking issues require a full English screenshot of the order page showing the address, tracking number, and order value.
  • • Address changes during shipment may incur additional handling fees and cannot be guaranteed successful.
  • • If tracking shows “Delivered” but the customer claims non-receipt, compensation is not available without signature service.

Damaged Items & Product Issues

  • • If the outer package or product is visibly damaged, customers should refuse delivery immediately.
  • • After signing, customers must provide: outer box photos, shipping label photos, and damage photos for claim evaluation.
  • • Depending on the damage severity, partial compensation, replacement, or full refund may be provided.
  • • TPE products may experience makeup fading during use. This is considered normal wear and not a product defect.
  • • Overseas warehouse dolls use standard skeleton structures. Excessive force or unnatural posing may damage the frame.
  • • Product quality issues must be reported within 7 days after delivery. Late claims may not be accepted.

Final Sale Policy

  • • Due to hygiene and privacy regulations, all intimate wellness products are final sale.
  • • No returns or exchanges are accepted without verified quality issues.
  • • For confirmed defects, compensation or replacement solutions may be offered.
  • • Unauthorized returns may not be accepted or refunded, especially if the item affects resale condition.
  • • Full silicone products are strictly non-returnable and non-exchangeable. Only compensation or replacement support is available for verified quality defects.
