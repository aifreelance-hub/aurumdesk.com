Privacy & Policy

Privacy Policy

We are committed to protecting your privacy in the strictest manner. All personal information you provide will only be used for order processing, shipping, and customer support.

We never sell, rent, or disclose your personal data to third parties for marketing or any other unauthorized purposes. Your identity, order history, and contact details remain 100% confidential.

Discreet Shipping

All orders are shipped in plain, unmarked packaging with no product labels, logos, or descriptions. The external packaging provides no indication of the contents.

Shipping labels contain no sensitive information, ensuring complete privacy for delivery to your home, office, or any address.

Shipping & Delivery

All items are IN STOCK in our USA warehouse. Orders are processed and shipped out within 24 hours on business days.

Delivery time is 3-5 business days across the US. No customs duties, no delays, no international risks.

Return & Warranty

Each product undergoes strict quality inspection before shipment.

If the item arrives damaged or defective, we offer a free replacement or full refund. For health and hygiene reasons, we do not accept returns for used items.
